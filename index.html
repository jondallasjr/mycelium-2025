Four Stages, 50 Acts, One Interconnected Musical Ecosystem

Mycelium 2025 transforms Tiger Jungle Art Camp into a temporary village at the foot of Chiang Dao's mountains where Thailand's underground music scene meets international electronic pioneers across three days of continuous programming.

The festival operates on a biological metaphor—beneath your feet, mycelial networks connect plants through fungal threads, enabling communication and mutual support across vast distances. This same philosophy drives the festival's curation: 50 artists spanning Thai reggae legends, Lanna electronic innovators, Bangkok techno architects, and Berlin atmospheric producers, all interconnected through sound.

The mycelial network: nature's internet connecting all living things

Four Distinct Sonic Territories

Mycelium Main Stage

Full-band performances showcasing rock, reggae, folk, and world music. Central gathering space for communal experiences.

Jungle Stage

DJ sets beneath forest canopy specializing in house, funk, downtempo from morning through dusk.

Psytrance Stage

Graveyard shift programming with forest psytrance and twilight crossovers spiraling until sunrise.

Grounding Stage

Ambient drift and experimental haze for introspective sonic journeys and contemplative spaces.

More Than Music
