Complete Artist Guide

50 acts across 4 stages. Decades of scene-building, cultural preservation, and creative risk-taking.

Mycelium 2025 showcases Thailand's underground music scene meeting international electronic pioneers. This comprehensive guide provides ratings, detailed profiles, and strategic scheduling intelligence to help you navigate 50 acts across 48+ hours of programming.

Rating System

⭐⭐⭐ Unmissable Performances - Pioneer status, unique positioning, or exceptional timing
⭐⭐ Highly Recommended - Scene architects and innovators
⭐ Worth Catching - Solid performances and emerging talents
🌙 Graveyard Shift - Performances between 02:00-08:00

Tier 1: Unmissable Performances ⭐⭐⭐

Srirajah Rockers

⭐⭐⭐ Saturday 23:00-00:00 Mycelium

22 Years of Thai Reggae Mastery

Who They Are: Thailand's premier 12-piece reggae/dub band delivering roots rock reggae with distinctive Thai flavor. Operating since 2003, with "Enduro" album released January 2025.

Festival Credentials: Performed at Wonderfruit Festival alongside Rudimental and Nightmares on Wax, Maho Rasop Festival with Tame Impala. Described as "the most popular band among young people in Thailand."

Why Unmissable: This is Thailand's reggae scene at its highest level. Saturday 23:00 timing positions them at peak festival energy when a 12-piece reggae orchestra delivers maximum communal celebration.

Easy Velvet

⭐⭐⭐ Saturday 16:00-18:00 Jungle

Sunset Downtempo Ethnic Fusion

Sound Signature: Downtempo ethnic music producer fusing trip hop, oriental vibes, and psychedelic modulations. Think DJ Shadow's cinematic approach meeting Asian instrumentation.

Why Unmissable: Perfect sunset timing (16:00-18:00) on Jungle Stage. As daylight fades into festival night, Easy Velvet's atmospheric downtempo provides the essential bridge between afternoon and evening energy.

Helicopter Secondhand

⭐⭐⭐ Friday 19:30-20:30 Mycelium

World Cup Busking Champions

Who They Are: Chiang Mai trio (Pod, Bas, Jack) who represented Thailand at Busking World Cup 2023 in South Korea. International competition recognition validates their street-performance roots and cross-cultural appeal.

Sound Identity: Fusion combining contemporary Western pop-rock with Aboriginal, African, South American, and Thai Lanna musical influences. Didgeridoo's drone paired with Lanna percussion creates unique sonic territory.

Why Unmissable: Friday 19:30 positions them as the festival's opening statement—perfect introduction to Chiang Mai's world-music sensibility.

Venn

⭐⭐⭐ Saturday 21:30-22:30 Mycelium

2024's Best Album Recognition

2024 Recognition: Second album "Mölkky" (November 2024) recognized by multiple Thai media outlets as one of the year's best albums. 12 songs, 54 minutes, bilingual Thai-English lyrics.

Festival Pedigree: Performed at Maho Rasop, Pelupo, and Wonderfruit—Thailand's major independent festivals.

Why Unmissable: Catching an award-worthy act at creative peak timing before larger recognition changes their festival positioning.

Amber Leaf

⭐⭐⭐ Friday 23:45-00:45 + Sunday 09:20-11:20 Grounding

Movement-Based Ambient Experience

Unique Approach: Creates music for embodied listening—crafted specifically for movement and dance within ambient contexts. Not passive background music.

Two Opportunities: Friday late night (23:45) and Sunday morning (09:20). Two completely different energy contexts for experiencing this approach.

Why Unmissable: Rare to encounter ambient music designed explicitly for physical engagement rather than contemplation.

Wannarit / Stylish Nonsense

⭐⭐⭐ 🌙 Friday 01:00-02:00 Grounding

32 Years of Pioneer Status

Legendary Credentials: One half of Stylish Nonsense, Thailand's experimental electronic duo operating since 1993. Founded Panda Records (2000) and created Stone Free Music Festival (2012-14).

Performance Philosophy: 100% improvisation. Each performance exists once, then disappears. Technical limitations as musicians forced electronic experimentation, becoming signature strength.

Why Unmissable: True pioneer status. Graveyard-shift timing (01:00) perfectly suits experimental ambient. He was crafting this music before most attendees were born.

Wuthichai

⭐⭐⭐ Sunday 11:20-13:20 Jungle

24 Years Integrating Lanna Folk & Modern Electronic

Pioneering Status: Chiang Mai electronic music pioneer (DJ Shaky) integrating traditional Lanna folk with modern electronic since 2001. Significantly influenced Chiang Mai's music scene.

Scene Architecture: Founded Jive Festival (14 annual editions pre-COVID), established Ga-Lae Recordings label (2023), co-founded Warm Up Cafe.

"Bai Mai Wai" Album: Translates to "The Story of My Hometown," capturing journey through Nepal and Thailand mountains, blending tribal voices into rich soundscape.

Why Unmissable: Essential for understanding Chiang Mai's musical evolution. His Lanna-electronic fusion provides cultural grounding as festival concludes.

Tier 2: Highly Recommended ⭐⭐

Makara

⭐⭐ Friday 21:00-22:00 Mycelium

Sound: Thai psychedelic rock with contemporary indie influences. Five-piece with improvisation focus—each live performance becomes unique.

Why Attend: Following Helicopter Secondhand's world-music fusion, Makara shifts toward psychedelic exploration. Value spontaneous creativity over predictable execution.

Liem

⭐⭐ Friday 22:00-00:00 Jungle

Sound: House music with deep grooves and melodic sensibilities. Bangkok-based DJ known for reading dancefloor energy.

Why Attend: Prime Friday night slot (22:00-00:00) on Jungle Stage for those wanting to dance rather than watch bands.

Pyrosoma

⭐⭐ 🌙 Friday 04:45-05:45 Grounding

Sound: Ambient techno exploring the space between contemplation and movement. Named after bioluminescent marine organisms.

Why Attend: Dawn slot (04:45) for those seeking transcendent sunrise experiences. Music designed for liminal states.

Elaheh

⭐⭐ 🌙 Saturday 02:00-04:00 Jungle

Scene Credentials: Bangkok-born DJ with Persian roots, integral to Bangkok's underground through UNST (techno institution) and Soundistan movement.

Sound Philosophy: "Deep, cosmic, and psychedelic" house/techno. Prime graveyard slot (02:00-04:00) for transcendent dancefloor journeys.

Shantiago

⭐⭐ Sunday 06:20-08:20 Psysquare

Unique Positioning: Spanish-born musician, DJ, and sound therapist. Progressive psytrance transitioning to ambient with sound healing focus.

Why Attend: Sunday sunrise bridge—maintains nighttime energy while introducing reflective comedown. Trained in using frequency for therapeutic purposes.

Decision-Making Framework

🏆 Top Priority (⭐⭐⭐)

  • Srirajah Rockers - Saturday 23:00
  • Easy Velvet - Saturday 16:00 (sunset)
  • Venn - Saturday 21:30
  • Helicopter Secondhand - Friday 19:30
  • Amber Leaf - Friday 23:45 OR Sunday 09:20
  • Wannarit - Friday 01:00 (graveyard)
  • Wuthichai - Sunday 11:20

Strategic Considerations

Sunset Slots: Easy Velvet (Saturday 16:00) captures the magical transition from day to night—often the most memorable festival moment.

Graveyard Shift (🌙): Acts performing 02:00-08:00 demand physical commitment. Attempt only if genuinely energized; forcing participation creates misery, not transcendence.

Stage Proximity: Mycelium and Jungle stages are walkable. Psysquare and Grounding require more intentional journey. Plan accordingly.

Cultural Context: Wuthichai (Sunday 11:20) provides essential cultural grounding—understand Chiang Mai's musical DNA before leaving.

Schedule Clashes to Navigate

Saturday 21:30-23:00: Venn (Mycelium) vs. Srirajah Rockers (Mycelium). Catch Venn, then transition to Srirajah at 23:00.

Friday Night: Multiple graveyard options. Choose based on energy: Wannarit (ambient), Hellblueboi (house), or Vbrtns (experimental).

Sunday Morning: Choose recovery path: Amber Leaf (09:20) for movement-ambient or Wuthichai (11:20) for Lanna-electronic.
