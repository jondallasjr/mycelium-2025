Complete Artist Guide

50 acts across 4 stages. Decades of scene-building, cultural preservation, and creative risk-taking.

Mycelium 2025 showcases Thailand's underground music scene meeting international electronic pioneers. This comprehensive guide provides ratings, detailed profiles, and strategic scheduling intelligence to help you navigate 50 acts across 48+ hours of programming.

Rating System

Unmissable Performances - Pioneer status, unique positioning, or exceptional timing
Highly Recommended - Scene architects and innovators
Worth Catching - Solid performances and emerging talents
Graveyard Shift - Performances between 02:00-08:00

Tier 1: Unmissable Performances

Srirajah Rockers

Saturday 23:00-00:00 Mycelium

22 Years of Thai Reggae Mastery

Who They Are: Thailand's premier 12-piece reggae/dub band delivering roots rock reggae with distinctive Thai flavor. Operating since 2003, with "Enduro" album released January 2025.

Why Unmissable: This is Thailand's reggae scene at its highest level. Saturday 23:00 timing positions them at peak festival energy when a 12-piece reggae orchestra delivers maximum communal celebration.

Easy Velvet

Saturday 16:00-18:00 Jungle

Sunset Downtempo Ethnic Fusion

Sound Signature: Downtempo ethnic music producer fusing trip hop, oriental vibes, and psychedelic modulations.

Why Unmissable: Perfect sunset timing (16:00-18:00) on Jungle Stage. As daylight fades into festival night, Easy Velvet's atmospheric downtempo provides the essential bridge between afternoon and evening energy.

Helicopter Secondhand

Friday 19:30-20:30 Mycelium

World Cup Busking Champions

Who They Are: Chiang Mai trio who represented Thailand at Busking World Cup 2023 in South Korea.

Why Unmissable: Friday 19:30 positions them as the festival's opening statement—perfect introduction to Chiang Mai's world-music sensibility.

Venn

Saturday 21:30-22:30 Mycelium

2024's Best Album Recognition

2024 Recognition: Second album "Mölkky" recognized by multiple Thai media outlets as one of the year's best albums.

Why Unmissable: Catching an award-worthy act at creative peak timing before larger recognition changes their festival positioning.

Amber Leaf

Friday 23:45-00:45 + Sunday 09:20-11:20 Grounding

Movement-Based Ambient Experience

Unique Approach: Creates music for embodied listening—crafted specifically for movement and dance within ambient contexts.

Why Unmissable: Rare to encounter ambient music designed explicitly for physical engagement rather than contemplation.
