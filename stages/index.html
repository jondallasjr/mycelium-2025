Strategic Scheduling Tips

🌅 Don't Miss Sunset

Easy Velvet (Saturday 16:00-18:00) captures the magical transition. Often the most memorable festival moment.

🌙 Graveyard Shift Strategy

Acts performing 02:00-08:00 demand physical commitment. Attempt only if genuinely energized. Forcing it creates misery.

🗺️ Stage Proximity

Mycelium and Jungle are walkable. Psysquare and Grounding require intentional journey. Plan transitions.

📋 Key Clashes

Saturday 21:30-23:00: Venn then Srirajah Rockers (both Mycelium). Catch Venn, transition at 23:00.
