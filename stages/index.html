Four Stages, Four Sonic Territories

Each stage creates distinct environmental micro-climates for different musical journeys.

Mycelium 2025's four stages operate as interconnected nodes in a living musical network. Each provides unique sonic environments, from full-band performances to experimental ambient drift, psytrance spirals to jungle canopy house sessions. Understanding each stage's character helps you navigate the festival's 48+ hours intelligently.

🎸

Mycelium Main Stage

Full-band performances • Rock • Reggae • Folk • World Music

The Heart of the Network

The Mycelium Main Stage serves as the festival's central gathering point, hosting full-band performances requiring complete instrumentation. This is where Srirajah Rockers' 12-piece reggae orchestra commands the space, where Helicopter Secondhand's world-music fusion captivates crowds, where Venn's experimental alternative folk unfolds across 54 minutes of bilingual storytelling.

Unlike electronic-focused stages, Mycelium celebrates acoustic instrumentation and live band chemistry. Expect drum kits, bass rigs, guitar amplifiers, horn sections, and genuine human interaction between musicians reading each other's energy in real-time.

Sound System & Capacity

Professional PA system designed for full-band dynamics—handling everything from intimate acoustic moments to reggae bass depth and rock crescendos. Large open area accommodating 500+ attendees for communal festival experiences.

Programming Philosophy

Curated for acts requiring traditional band setups and audience attention to musicianship. Prime evening slots (19:00-00:00) when festival energy peaks and communal gathering feels natural.

Featured Artists

  • Srirajah Rockers - 22-year Thai reggae legends (Saturday 23:00)
  • Helicopter Secondhand - World Cup busking champions (Friday 19:30)
  • Venn - 2024's best album recognition (Saturday 21:30)
  • Makara - Psychedelic rock improvisation (Friday 21:00)

Mycelium Main Stage: Central gathering point for full-band performances

Jungle Stage

DJ Sets • House • Funk • Downtempo • Atmospheric Electronic

Beneath the Forest Canopy

The Jungle Stage operates beneath Chiang Dao's forest canopy, creating an intimate sonic environment where trees become natural acoustic diffusers. DJ sets run from morning through dusk, specializing in house, funk, downtempo, and atmospheric electronic—genres that breathe with the jungle's organic rhythms.

This stage captures the festival's sunset moment: Easy Velvet's downtempo ethnic fusion (Saturday 16:00-18:00) as daylight transforms into evening energy, providing the essential transition between afternoon exploration and nighttime intensity.

Natural Acoustics

Forest canopy provides shade and unique acoustic properties. Sound moves differently through jungle vegetation—softer, more enveloping, creating intimate listening experiences despite outdoor setting.

Programming Philosophy

Morning yoga sessions transition to midday house grooves, afternoon downtempo, sunset magic hour, and deep nighttime journeys. Programming emphasizes natural energy cycles—wake up, explore, dance, transcend.

Featured Artists

  • Easy Velvet - Sunset downtempo ethnic fusion (Saturday 16:00)
  • Wuthichai - 24 years Lanna-electronic integration (Sunday 11:20)
  • Liem - House grooves (Friday 22:00)
  • Elaheh - Deep cosmic psychedelic (Saturday 02:00)

Golden hour at Jungle Stage: Where sunset meets sound

Psytrance Stage (Psysquare)

Forest Psytrance • Twilight Crossovers • Dark Psychedelic

Graveyard Shift Specialists

Psysquare operates through the night's deepest hours, spiraling until sunrise with forest psytrance, twilight crossovers, and dark psychedelic. This stage demands physical commitment—those who arrive at 02:00 must genuinely want transcendent dancefloor experiences, not casual encounters.

The graveyard shift (02:00-08:00) requires specific energy states. Psysquare respects this intensity, programming artists who understand how to guide dancers through darkness toward dawn. Shantiago's Sunday sunrise set (06:20-08:20) demonstrates this expertise: progressive psytrance transitioning to ambient with sound healing focus.

Dedicated Psychedelic Space

Isolated from other stages to prevent sound bleed and maintain immersive atmosphere. Designed for those seeking continuous psychedelic journeys rather than stage-hopping variety.

Programming Philosophy

Night becomes day becomes transformation. Forest psytrance builds through twilight hours, peaks in darkness, then gently releases into sunrise ambient. Complete arcs, not fragmented sets.

Featured Artists

  • Shantiago - Sound healing sunrise bridge (Sunday 06:20)
  • Various psytrance artists - Graveyard shift specialists throughout weekend

🌙 Graveyard Energy Required

This stage isn't for casual visits. Come prepared for deep journeys, extended dancing, and surrendering to the night's arc. Forcing graveyard participation creates misery—only attend if genuinely energized.

🧘

Grounding Stage

Ambient Drift • Experimental Haze • Contemplative Soundscapes

Refuge and Laboratory

The Grounding Stage serves dual purposes: refuge for overwhelmed nervous systems and laboratory for adventurous ears. Programming emphasizes texture, atmosphere, and contemplative spaces—ambient drift and experimental haze for introspective sonic journeys.

Here you'll find Wannarit (Stylish Nonsense) delivering 100% improvised experimental electronics at Friday 01:00, his 32 years of pioneer status manifesting in pure spontaneous creation. Amber Leaf's movement-based ambient appears twice (Friday 23:45 and Sunday 09:20), offering music crafted for embodied listening rather than passive reception.

Intimate Listening Environment

Smaller capacity, cushioned seating areas, lower lighting. Designed for sitting, lying down, moving slowly, or simply existing within sound rather than demanding attention.

Programming Philosophy

Sound as healing, sound as exploration, sound as space itself. No predictable buildups or drops—just sustained atmospheres that reveal themselves gradually to patient listeners.

Featured Artists

  • Wannarit / Stylish Nonsense - 32-year pioneer (Friday 01:00)
  • Amber Leaf - Movement-based ambient (Friday 23:45 + Sunday 09:20)
  • Pyrosoma - Ambient techno dawn (Friday 04:45)
  • Midnight Valencia - Experimental haze (Friday 02:15)

🌿 Intentional Listening

This stage rewards attention and patience. Not background music—foreground experience for those willing to listen deeply. Perfect for comedowns, resets, or genuine sonic exploration.

Stage Layout & Proximity

Festival layout showing interconnected stages and mycelial pathways

Navigation Strategy

Close Proximity: Mycelium and Jungle stages are easily walkable (5 minutes). You can catch sets on both without missing beats.

Intentional Journey: Psysquare and Grounding stages require more deliberate travel (10-15 minutes). Plan accordingly—these aren't casual drop-ins.

Sound Management: Stages positioned to minimize sound bleed. Each maintains distinct sonic territory without overlap interference.
